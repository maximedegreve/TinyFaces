Farewell, friends

TinyFaces was a free collection of real avatars, submitted by designers to use in their work. It's now offline for good, along with our Sketch and Figma plugins, since AI can now make a unique face in seconds. But seeing the real faces of people I knew show up in designs will always be remembered.

At its peak more than 50k designers were using TinyFaces. That still amazes me. Thank you to everyone who used it and sent in their faces. The code is still on GitHub, and nft.tinyfac.es will just continue.
